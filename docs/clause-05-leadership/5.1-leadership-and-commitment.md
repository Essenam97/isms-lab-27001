# 5.1 Leadership and commitment

Clause 5.1 asks top management to show leadership and commitment to the ISMS. At Lagune Payroll, top management is the management team: the CEO, the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and the Chief Operating Officer (COO).

Top management can delegate work on the ISMS, but not its accountability. The ISMS manager drafts most documents; the CEO approves them and answers for them to auditors and customers.

## How top management meets each requirement

The left column paraphrases clause 5.1 a) to h).

| Requirement (paraphrased) | What top management does at Lagune Payroll | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| a) Policy and objectives set and compatible with the company's strategy | Approves the [information security policy](5.2-information-security-policy.md) and the yearly objectives; links them to the goal of winning tenders from large customers (EXT-04) | Signed policy; management review minutes |
| b) ISMS requirements built into business processes | Security steps added to existing processes: release pipeline, HR onboarding and offboarding, customer support, supplier purchasing | Procedures in the risk treatment plan (A05, A07, A09, A10) |
| c) Resources available | Approves the budget of the risk treatment plan (5 750 000 XOF) and 40% of the ISMS manager's time | Kick-off meeting minutes; budget line in the 2027 budget |
| d) Importance of security communicated | CEO presents the policy to all staff at the kick-off; repeated at each onboarding | Attendance list; onboarding checklist |
| e) ISMS achieves its intended outcomes | Reviews ISMS performance at each management review | Management review minutes (clause 9.3) |
| f) People directed and supported | Managers make their teams' security tasks part of normal work; late actions discussed at monthly management meetings | Monthly meeting notes |
| g) Continual improvement promoted | Corrective actions tracked and reviewed (clause 10) | Nonconformity and corrective action log |
| h) Other managers supported in their area | Each head of team owns the risks of their area (see risk owners in the [risk method](../clause-06-planning/6.1.2-risk-assessment-method.md)) | Signed residual risk acceptance in the [risk treatment plan](../clause-06-planning/6.1.3-risk-treatment-plan.md) |
