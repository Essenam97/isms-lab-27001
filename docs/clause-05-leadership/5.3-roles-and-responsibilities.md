# 5.3 Organizational roles, responsibilities and authorities

Clause 5.3 asks top management to assign responsibilities and authorities for the ISMS, in particular for making sure the ISMS meets the standard and for reporting on its performance to top management.

## Organization

| Role | Holder | Reports to |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | CEO | Shareholders |
| CTO | CTO | CEO |
| COO | COO | CEO |
| ISMS manager | Head of Infrastructure (40% of her time) | CEO for ISMS matters, CTO for infrastructure work |
| Head of Development | Head of Development | CTO |
| Head of Infrastructure | Head of Infrastructure | CTO |
| Head of Customer Support | Head of Customer Support | COO |
| HR Manager | HR Manager | CEO |
| Internal auditor | Person outside the infrastructure team, or an external auditor (A17) | CEO |
| All staff | Employees, interns, contractors | Their manager |

## Responsibilities and authorities

| Role | Responsibilities | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | Accountable for the ISMS; approves policy, scope, objectives, budget; accepts residual risks R06 and R13; chairs the management review | Final decision on all ISMS matters |
| CTO and COO | Make sure their departments carry out the risk treatment plan; attend the management review | Assign staff and time in their departments |
| ISMS manager | Keeps the ISMS documents current; runs the risk assessment; maintains the SoA; tracks the risk treatment plan monthly; reports ISMS performance to the CEO every month and at the management review; coordinates incident response | Can ask any team for information and evidence; can escalate late actions directly to the CEO |
| Risk owners (heads of team, HR Manager, CEO) | Decide on treatment of their risks; carry out their actions; accept residual risks | Approve or refuse changes that affect their risks |
| Internal auditor | Plans and carries out internal audits; reports findings to the CEO | Access to all ISMS records and staff within the audit scope |
| Managers | Apply the policy in their teams; make sure staff attend awareness sessions; request and remove access for their staff | Approve access requests for their team |
| All staff | Follow the policy and topic-specific policies; report incidents and weaknesses | Stop a task they believe is unsafe and report it |

The CEO checks whether Law No. 2019-014 requires Lagune Payroll to appoint a data protection officer (action A16). Until this is decided, the CEO is the contact for data protection questions.

## Independence of the ISMS manager

The ISMS manager is also Head of Infrastructure, and she owns 7 of the 13 risks. Two measures limit the conflict of interest:

1. She reports on the ISMS directly to the CEO, not to the CTO.
2. The internal audit of infrastructure controls is carried out by someone outside her team (A17), so she never audits her own work.
