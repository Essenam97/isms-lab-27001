## 1. Purpose

Lagune Payroll SARL stores the identity data, salaries and bank details of the employees of about 180 Togolese companies. Our customers pay their staff through Lagune Pay. If this data leaks, is altered or is unavailable on payroll days, real people lose money or privacy. This policy sets out how we protect it.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all activities, people, information and systems inside the ISMS scope defined in [4.3](../clause-04-context/4.3-isms-scope.md). It applies to all employees, interns and contractors who work on those activities.

## 3. What we protect

- Confidentiality: payroll and personal data is seen only by people who need it for their work.
- Integrity: salaries and bank details are changed only by authorized people, and every change can be traced.
- Availability: Lagune Pay is available to customers, in particular during the payroll window from the 25th of each month to the 5th of the next.

## 4. Commitments

Management commits to:

1. meet the legal, regulatory and contractual requirements that apply to information security, including Law No. 2019-014 on personal data protection and Law No. 2018-026 on cybersecurity and cybercrime, and the security clauses of customer contracts;
2. manage information security on the basis of risk, using the method in [6.1.2](../clause-06-planning/6.1.2-risk-assessment-method.md);
3. provide the people, time and budget the ISMS needs;
4. improve the ISMS continually, using audit results, incidents and measurements.

## 5. Information security objectives

Management sets information security objectives each year at the management review. Each objective must be consistent with this policy, measurable where possible, have an owner and a deadline, and take into account the results of the risk assessment. Objectives are recorded in the objectives document (clause 6.2) and monitored at each management review.

## 6. Rules for everyone

- Use only your own account; never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Do not store customer data on laptops, USB drives or personal phones, and do not send it by personal email or messaging apps.
- Lock your screen when you leave your desk.
- Report any suspected incident, phishing email or lost device at once to the security reporting channel. Reporting a mistake you made yourself will not be punished.
- Follow the topic-specific policies that apply to your work.
