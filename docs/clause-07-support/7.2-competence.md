# 7.2 Competence

Clause 7.2 asks the organization to determine the competence needed by people whose work affects information security, make sure they have it, act where they do not, and keep documented evidence.

| Role | Competence needed | Evidence held | Gap | Action and date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ISMS manager | Knowledge of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and its Annex A; risk assessment; running audits and management reviews | ISO/IEC 27001 Foundation certificate | No implementation training | ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer training and exam by 2027-03-31, from the HR training budget |
| Internal auditor (IA-2027-01) | Audit principles and methods (ISO 19011); knowledge of ISO/IEC 27001:2022; independence from the areas audited | None in the company | No qualified internal auditor | External auditor with an ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor certificate, under action A17 |
| Risk owners | The risk method (6.1.2); their role in treatment and acceptance | None | Not trained | 2-hour session on the risk method by the ISMS manager by 2026-11-30, with attendance list |
| Developers | Secure coding; the rules of POL-02 | None | Not trained | Secure coding session every year (POL-02 section 8), first by 2027-02-28 |
| Infrastructure team | Administration of Keycloak, Wazuh and the backup tools | Work experience, not documented | No record | Record of each person's training and experience by 2026-12-15 |
| All staff | Awareness of the policy and of their duties (clause 7.3) | None | Covered by action A14 | Onboarding session and phishing awareness (A14) |

Evidence (certificates, attendance lists, training records) is kept by the HR Manager for the duration of employment plus 3 years.
