# 7.5 Documented information

Clause 7.5 asks the organization to keep the documented information that the standard and the ISMS need, and to control its creation, update, approval, distribution, storage, change and retention.

## 1. Types and who approves them

| Type | Examples | Approved by | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISMS documents | Scope, context, risk method, Statement of Applicability, risk treatment plan, objectives | CEO | At least once a year |
| Policies | Information security policy, POL-01 to POL-09 | CEO | At least once a year |
| Procedures and playbooks | Incident playbooks, restore procedure, offboarding checklist | Owner named in the related policy | At least once a year |
| Records | Minutes, audit reports, access reviews, test reports, evidence files, incident records | Not approved; written by the person who did the work | Not reviewed; kept as written |

## 2. Writing and changing a document

1. Every document has an owner, a version table and a status line.
2. The master copy is kept in the company's Git repository, which records every change with its author and date.
3. A change produces a new version in draft (for example 1.1). The status line says draft until approval.
4. Approval is given in a meeting or by a signed message, and recorded in the approval register below with the version, the date and the approver. The document's version table and status line are updated at the same time.
5. A change of more than wording, to an approved document, is approved again.

## 3. Publication

1. Approved policies and procedures are published to staff in Nextcloud, folder "ISMS - Approved", as PDF exports of the approved version, within 5 working days of approval.
2. Staff are told by email when a policy they must follow is published or changed.
3. Earlier versions stay in Git history and are removed from the Nextcloud folder.

## 4. Protection and retention

1. The Git repository and the Nextcloud folder follow POL-01 for access and POL-03 for handling. ISMS documents are Internal unless they state another class.
2. Records are kept for 3 years, unless the register of legal and contractual requirements (action A16) sets another period.
