## 1. Purpose

This policy sets what staff may and may not do with the company's information, accounts and equipment.

## 2. Company accounts and tools

1. Company accounts are for company work. Limited personal use of a company laptop is allowed if it does not break the rules below.
2. Customer data is handled only in Lagune Pay and Nextcloud, following POL-03.
3. Software is installed on company laptops only from the list kept by the infrastructure team, or after its approval.
4. Staff must not turn off or bypass security tools: disk encryption, malware protection, screen lock, DNS filtering, updates.

## 3. Forbidden uses

- Sharing an account, a password or an MFA code with anyone, including a colleague or a manager.
- Copying Confidential information to a USB drive, a personal device, a personal cloud account, a personal email address or a messaging app.
- Using a company account to sign up for services not approved by the infrastructure team.
- Trying to access information or systems without authorization, including "to test" them.

## 4. At the desk and outside the office

1. Lock the screen when leaving the desk. Screens lock by themselves after 5 minutes without activity.
2. Do not leave papers with Internal or Confidential information on the desk at the end of the day.
3. Outside the office, keep the laptop with you or locked away; never leave it visible in a vehicle. Remote work follows POL-05.

## 5. Email, links and attachments

1. Check the sender before opening a link or an attachment. Requests to change bank details, to pay urgently or to give a password are treated as suspicious, even from a known name.
2. Report suspicious messages to the security reporting channel without forwarding them to colleagues.

## 6. Reporting

Lost or stolen equipment, a suspicious message, a mistake with Confidential data or any other security event is reported at once to the security reporting channel (POL-08). Reporting a mistake you made yourself is not punished.

## 7. Return of equipment

Laptops, badges and any other company equipment are returned on the last working day, with a signed record (action A05).

## 8. Monitoring

Company systems record logins, administrator actions and security events (POL-01, section 8). These records are used for security only, and access to them is limited to the infrastructure team and the ISMS manager.

## Exceptions

An exception to this policy must be written, justified, limited in time, approved by the CEO and recorded in the exception register kept by the ISMS manager.

## Review of this policy

At least once a year, and after any incident or change that shows it no longer fits.
