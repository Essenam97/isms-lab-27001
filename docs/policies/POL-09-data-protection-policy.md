## 1. Purpose

Lagune Pay holds the identity data, salaries and bank details of thousands of employees of Togolese companies. This policy sets how Lagune Payroll meets Law No. 2019-014 of 29 October 2019 on the protection of personal data, and its contracts with customers.

## 2. Roles

1. For the payroll data of its customers' employees, Lagune Payroll processes data on behalf of each customer, under its instructions and contract. For its own staff and contacts, Lagune Payroll decides how data is processed.
2. The CEO is the contact for data protection until the company decides whether it must appoint a data protection officer (action A16).

## 3. Principles

Personal data is processed only:

1. for a defined purpose, set out in the register of processing;
2. when there is a legal basis, such as the customer contract or the consent of the person;
3. to the extent needed for that purpose;
4. for as long as needed, then deleted (section 6);
5. with security measures suited to the risk (the ISMS).

## 4. Register and declarations

1. The CEO keeps a register of processing: purpose, categories of data and people, recipients, transfers abroad, retention, security measures.
2. Processing is declared to the IPDCP as the law requires (action A16).
3. Transfers of personal data outside Togo, including to the production hosting provider, are documented with the analysis that the destination ensures an adequate level of protection, as the law requires for such transfers.

## 5. Rights of the people concerned

1. Requests for information, access, correction, objection or deletion are sent to the CEO and recorded.
2. For customers' employees, Lagune Payroll forwards the request to the customer and helps it answer, as the contract sets.
3. The procedure and the answer times are set in the data subject request procedure (action A16).

## 6. Retention and deletion

1. Retention periods for each category of data are set in the register of legal and contractual requirements.
2. At the end of a customer contract, the customer's data is returned and then deleted, and the deletion is recorded.

## 7. Privacy in the product

1. New features that process personal data are assessed for privacy before development (POL-02, section 2).
2. Support staff see bank and identity numbers masked (POL-01, section 7).
3. Test environments use fake data only (POL-02, section 6).

## 8. Breaches

Personal data breaches are handled under POL-08, including notification of the IPDCP and of customers.

## Exceptions

An exception to this policy must be written, justified, limited in time, approved by the CEO and recorded in the exception register kept by the ISMS manager.

## Review of this policy

At least once a year, and after any incident or change that shows it no longer fits.
