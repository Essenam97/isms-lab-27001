# Internal audit report IA-2026-01 (gap assessment)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-10-16 |
| Objective | Find what Lagune Payroll must still do, or correct, before the ISMS kick-off on 2026-11-04 and before certification |
| Criteria | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 with Amendment 1 (2024); Lagune Payroll's own ISMS documents and policies |
| Scope | Clauses 4 to 10; policies POL-01 to POL-09; lab evidence for actions A04, A07, A10, A11, A13 |
| Method | Review of documents and of lab evidence in this repository. No interviews and no site visit: the company is fictional |
| Auditor | Repository owner; impartiality limited (see the [audit programme](9.2-internal-audit-programme.md)) |
| Documents reviewed | All files under `docs/`, `lab/` and `evidence/` at the date of the audit |

## How findings are classified

| Class | Meaning in this audit |
|---|---|
| Nonconformity (NC) | A requirement of the standard or of Lagune Payroll's own documents is not met, and nothing in the ISMS plans to meet it (no owner, no date), or two documents contradict each other so that one cannot be followed |
| Observation (OBS) | A requirement is not met yet but is planned with an owner and a date, or a control has a weakness that could lead to a nonconformity |
| Opportunity for improvement (OFI) | Not a gap; a change that would make the ISMS easier to run or to audit |

Because the ISMS has not started yet, many requirements are "not met yet". Most of these are observations, not nonconformities, when the risk treatment plan covers them.

What works well: every Annex A control in the SoA traces back to a risk, an issue or a legal requirement; every Planned or Partial control is covered by an action with an owner, a date and a cost (checked by script when the plan was written); lab tests that passed without proving their result were found by review, rejected as evidence and corrected (Keycloak tests KC-02 and KC-04 in run 1, Nextcloud test NC-01, Wazuh test WZ-05).

## Nonconformities

### NC-01 Retention of Keycloak events: the policy is too short for the review, and the configuration does not show what applies

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | POL-01 sections 8.1 and 8.3; ISO/IEC 27001 clause 8.1 (planned processes must be workable) |
| Evidence | POL-01 section 8.1 keeps login events and administrator events for 90 days. `lab/keycloak/realm-lagune.json` sets `eventsExpiration` to 7776000 seconds (90 days), which applies to login events, and sets no retention for administrator events. POL-01 section 8.3 requires a quarterly review that uses the administrator events to find accounts on which MFA was removed |
| Finding | If administrator events are kept 90 days, as POL-01 says, events from the first days of a quarter (up to 92 days long) are deleted before the review after the quarter can read them. The configuration does not set their retention at all, and the lab evidence does not show how long Keycloak keeps them with this configuration. Either way, the review POL-01 asks for cannot be shown to be possible |
| Effect | This review is the detective control that covers OBS-01 |

### NC-02 The backup policy asks for more than the risk treatment plan provides

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | ISO/IEC 27001 clauses 6.1.3 e) and 6.2 (what will be done, with which resources) |
| Evidence | POL-06 section 2 sets a maximum data loss of 4 hours during the payroll window, and section 3.1 a backup every 4 hours in that window. Action A08 in the risk treatment plan covers a copy to a second provider, quarterly restore tests and a recovery plan, with a cost of 1 200 000 XOF; it does not mention backups every 4 hours |
| Finding | The 4-hour backups have no action, owner or budget. The cost of A08 may be too low |

### NC-03 No information security objectives

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | ISO/IEC 27001 clause 6.2 |
| Evidence | The information security policy (section 5) says objectives are set each year at the management review and recorded in a clause 6.2 document. The README lists objectives as planned. No objectives exist, and no date is set: the kick-off agenda (5.1) sets the date of the first management review, not of the objectives |
| Finding | The ISMS has no measurable objectives and no date to get them |

### NC-04 Monitoring and measurement are not defined

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | ISO/IEC 27001 clause 9.1 (what to monitor, methods, when, who, when results are analysed) |
| Evidence | The weekly workflows test controls in the lab and the evidence registers record the reviewed runs. No document says what Lagune Payroll measures, who analyses the results, or when. The registers say "a failed scheduled run must be added here" but name nobody to check the scheduled runs |
| Finding | Measurement happens in practice in the lab but is not defined as an ISMS process |

### NC-05 Competence of ISMS roles is not determined

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | ISO/IEC 27001 clause 7.2 |
| Evidence | Roles are defined in 5.3. No document states the competence needed for the ISMS manager, the internal auditor or the risk owners, nor keeps evidence of it. Action A14 covers awareness for all staff (clause 7.3), not competence |
| Finding | Competence is neither determined nor planned |

### NC-06 Rules for documented information are not written

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | ISO/IEC 27001 clause 7.5 |
| Evidence | Documents carry a version table and a status, and Git keeps their history. No document sets who approves which document, how approval is recorded, where approved versions are published to staff, or how long records are kept. All documents show "draft" and the approval column of 5.2 is empty |
| Finding | The practice exists but the rules do not, so approval cannot be checked against a standard |

## Observations

### OBS-01 MFA can be removed from an account by an administrator

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | POL-01 section 3: MFA "cannot be removed from an account except through an approved exception" |
| Evidence | Evidence A04, run 2, note 3: test KC-04 removed the MFA required action from a user through the admin API, and the user then logged in with a password alone (HTTP 200) |
| Finding | The tool does not enforce the policy; only the quarterly review would detect a removal, and NC-01 weakens that review |
| Recommendation | Make the Keycloak authentication flow require the one-time code at every login, so that MFA no longer depends on a required action, and add a test for it |

### OBS-02 Tests show MFA enrolment, not MFA at each login

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | POL-01 section 3; SoA control 8.5 |
| Evidence | KC-02 shows that a new user cannot get a token before setting up MFA. No test logs in a user who has set up MFA and checks that the code is asked again |
| Finding | Part of the policy is not covered by evidence |

### OBS-03 Single sign-on is not in place for Gitea and Nextcloud

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | POL-01 section 3: all internal tools are accessed through Keycloak |
| Evidence | `lab/gitea/README.md` and `lab/nextcloud/README.md`: both use local accounts; MFA from Keycloak is not tested there |
| Finding | Planned in action A04 (due 2027-01); not met yet |

### OBS-04 Automated security tests are not in the pipeline

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | POL-02 section 5 |
| Evidence | Gitea tests GT-01 to GT-06 cover review rules only. SoA controls 8.8 and 8.29 are Planned |
| Finding | Planned in action A10 (due 2027-04); not met yet. Until then, one reviewer is the only check on a change (see the design decisions in `lab/gitea/README.md`) |

### OBS-05 Log collection from Keycloak to Wazuh is not in place

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | POL-01 section 8.2: failed logins raise alerts |
| Evidence | Evidence A11: the rules work on real Keycloak lines, but the lines are sent by the test script through the logtest API, not collected by Wazuh |
| Finding | The detection logic is shown; the delivery of logs is not. Planned in action A11 (due 2027-03) |

### OBS-06 The register of legal requirements does not exist, and two policies depend on it

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | SoA control 5.31; POL-08 section 5; POL-09 sections 4 and 6 |
| Evidence | POL-08 leaves the time limit for notifying the IPDCP to the register. POL-09 leaves retention periods to it. The register is part of action A16 (due 2027-02) |
| Finding | Planned, but until it exists the company does not know whether the law sets a time limit for notifying a personal data breach. Risk R06 is rated High. This action should come first among the legal work |

### OBS-07 All ISMS documents are unapproved

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | ISO/IEC 27001 clauses 5.2, 6.1.3 f) and 6.1.2 (approval of the policy, the plan and residual risks) |
| Evidence | Every document under `docs/` has status draft |
| Finding | Planned at the kick-off meeting on 2026-11-04 (5.1, agenda items 1, 2 and 5). The kick-off agenda does not list the 9 topic-specific policies, which were written after it |

## Opportunities for improvement

| ID | Subject | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| OFI-01 | Consistency of references | The action numbers for each policy differ between 5.2 section 7 and the policy headers (for example POL-01: A04, A07 in 5.2; A04 to A07 in the policy). Keep one source |
| OFI-02 | Lab results and the plan | The risk treatment plan shows every action as "Not started" even where the lab has validated the design (A04, A07, A10, A11, A13). A column "design tested in lab" would show this without changing the SoA |
| OFI-03 | Test coverage | Add tests for: MFA asked at each login (OBS-02), the "CONFIDENTIAL" folder naming rule of POL-03, and repeated failures on one account spread over time (the Wazuh rules count all users together) |
| OFI-04 | Reviewer of evidence | The evidence files are reviewed by the person who wrote the tests. When a second person is available, have them review and sign the evidence |

## Conclusion of the auditor

The ISMS design is complete and traceable from context to controls, and four control areas have real, repeatable lab evidence. Before the kick-off, NC-02, NC-03 and NC-06 should be corrected and the topic-specific policies added to the kick-off agenda (OBS-07). Before certification, all six nonconformities need closed corrective actions, and the observations need to be either met or still within their planned dates.

The corrective actions are tracked in the [nonconformity log](../clause-10-improvement/10.2-nonconformity-log.md).
