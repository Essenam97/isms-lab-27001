# 9.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation

Clause 9.1 asks the organization to decide what needs to be monitored and measured, the methods, when it is done, who does it, and when and by whom the results are analysed and evaluated.

## What is measured

| What | Method and source | Frequency | Measured by | Analysed by | Reported to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lab control tests (Keycloak, Gitea, Nextcloud, Wazuh) | Scheduled GitHub Actions workflows; result recorded in the evidence registers | Every Monday | Workflows, automatically | ISMS manager, the same day: each failed run is entered in its register with a cause within 5 working days (OBJ-04) | Monthly ISMS report |
| Staff access rights and MFA | Quarterly access review (POL-01 section 8) | Every quarter | Risk owners | ISMS manager | Monthly ISMS report; management review |
| Backups | Backup alerts (POL-06 section 3) and restore test reports (POL-06 section 4) | Daily alerts; restore test every quarter | Infrastructure team | Head of Infrastructure | Monthly ISMS report; management review |
| Security alerts | Wazuh alerts of level 10 and above | Continuous; summary every month | Wazuh | ISMS manager | Monthly ISMS report |
| Incidents | Incident records (POL-08) | Every incident; summary every month | Incident lead | ISMS manager | Monthly ISMS report; management review |
| Risk treatment plan | Status of each action (Not started, In progress, Done, Late) | Every month | Action owners | ISMS manager | Monthly ISMS report to the CEO |
| Objectives OBJ-01 to OBJ-06 | Measures listed in 6.2 | Every month | Objective owners | ISMS manager | Monthly ISMS report; management review |
| Nonconformities | Nonconformity log (10.2) | Every month | ISMS manager | CEO | Monthly ISMS report; management review |

## Monthly ISMS report

The ISMS manager sends a report to the CEO in the first week of each month, with the results of the previous month for every line above. Reports are kept as records (7.5). The first report covers November 2026.

## Results as evidence

Only results with a date and a source are used: a workflow run link, a signed review record, a test report. Results that do not show why a test passed are not used as evidence (see the review notes in `evidence/`).
