# 9.2 Internal audit programme

Clause 9.2 asks the organization to audit its ISMS at planned intervals, with a programme that sets frequency, methods, responsibilities and reporting, and with auditors chosen so that the audit is objective and impartial.

## Programme

| Audit | Planned date | Type | Scope | Auditor | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IA-2026-01 | 2026-10-16 | Gap assessment before the ISMS kick-off | Clauses 4 to 10, the 9 topic-specific policies, and the controls tested in the lab | Repository owner (see limitation) | Done: [report](9.2-internal-audit-report-IA-2026-01.md) |
| IA-2027-01 | 2027-07 | Full internal audit before stage 1 certification (action A17) | Whole ISMS, all applicable Annex A controls, interviews and records | Person outside the infrastructure team, or external auditor | Planned |
| Yearly after certification | Every 12 months | Internal audit | Whole ISMS over the year, by sample | Same rule | Planned |

## Rules

1. Every audit has a written plan: objective, criteria, scope, method, sample.
2. No auditor audits their own work. The ISMS manager is not the auditor of infrastructure controls (5.3, independence of the ISMS manager).
3. Findings are classified as nonconformity, observation or opportunity for improvement (definitions in each report). Every nonconformity goes into the [nonconformity log](../clause-10-improvement/10.2-nonconformity-log.md).
4. Results are reported to the CEO and presented at the next management review.

## Limitation of IA-2026-01

The repository owner wrote most of the documents audited, so IA-2026-01 does not meet the impartiality that clause 9.2 requires. It is kept as a training exercise and as a gap assessment. It does not replace IA-2027-01.
