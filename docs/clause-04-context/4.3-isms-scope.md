# 4.3 Determining the scope of the ISMS

## Scope statement

The ISMS of Lagune Payroll SARL covers the development, operation and support of the Lagune Pay payroll and HR software-as-a-service, including the people, processes, information and IT systems used for these activities, at the Lomé office and in the production environment hosted by NimbusHost.

## In scope

- Processes: software development, release and change management, production operations, customer support, access management, incident management, backup and recovery, supplier management for IT providers, HR processes for in-scope staff (onboarding, training, offboarding).
- Teams: development, infrastructure and operations, customer support, management, HR (for staff security processes).
- Location: Lomé office.
- Systems: Lagune Pay production and test environments; Keycloak, Nextcloud, Gitea, Wazuh; staff laptops; office network.
- Information: customer and data-subject data in Lagune Pay; source code; credentials and keys; internal documentation.

## Out of scope

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| Sales and marketing activities (CRM, website, social media) | Do not process customer payroll data. The public website is a static site with no customer data. Planned for inclusion in year 2. |
| Company accounting software | Run by an external accountant on its own systems; covered by a supplier agreement only. |

## Interfaces and dependencies

| Interface | Party | What crosses it | How it is controlled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production hosting | NimbusHost | Servers, storage, network, physical security of the data centre | Supplier contract, provider's own certifications, 5.23 |
| Salary payment files | Customers' banks and mobile money operators | Files exported by customers from Lagune Pay | File integrity checks, 8.24 |
| Sales team | Internal, out of scope | Customer contact details only, no payroll data | No access to Lagune Pay production; checked in access reviews |
| Email | Hosted email provider | Internal and customer communications | Supplier assessment, 5.19 to 5.22 |

## Physical security of the data centre

Controls 7.1 to 7.14 apply to the Lomé office. For the data centre they are the cloud provider's responsibility; Lagune Payroll checks them through supplier evaluation and does not exclude them from the SoA.
