# 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties

Clause 4.2 asks the organization to determine the interested parties relevant to the ISMS, their requirements, and which of those requirements the ISMS will address.

| ID | Interested party | Requirements | Addressed by the ISMS? | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IP-01 | Customers (SME employers) | Confidentiality of payroll data; service available on payroll days (25th to 5th of each month); notice of incidents affecting their data | Yes | Risk register, 5.24 to 5.27, 8.13, 8.14 |
| IP-02 | Large customers in tenders | Evidence of an ISMS; answers to security questionnaires; right to audit | Yes | Certification, SoA, supplier clauses |
| IP-03 | Employees of customers (data subjects) | Their data is accurate, protected and not misused; they can exercise their rights | Yes | 5.34, data subject request procedure |
| IP-04 | IPDCP (data protection authority) | Declaration of processing; compliance with Law 2019-014 | Yes | 5.31, 5.34 |
| IP-05 | Lagune Payroll staff | Clear rules; tools that work; privacy of their own HR data | Yes | 6.3, policies, 5.34 |
| IP-06 | Management and shareholders | Win tenders; avoid fines and reputational damage; controlled cost | Yes | ISMS objectives (6.2) |
| IP-07 | Cloud provider (NimbusHost) | Customer follows the acceptable use policy and shared responsibility model | Yes | 5.23 |
| IP-08 | Banks and mobile money operators | Salary payment files are correct and not altered | Yes | 8.24, integrity controls on export files |
| IP-09 | Certification body | Access to evidence and staff during audits | Yes | 7.5, audit planning |
| IP-10 | Neighbours of the office | No direct requirement on information security | No | n/a |

Requirements marked "Yes" are inputs to the risk assessment (6.1.2) and to the Statement of Applicability.

Amendment 1 (2024) adds a note that relevant interested parties can have requirements related to climate change. None were identified in this version; to be checked again at the next review.
