# 6.1.3 d) Statement of Applicability

Clause 6.1.3 d) requires a Statement of Applicability (SoA) that lists the necessary controls, the justification for including them, whether they are implemented, and the justification for excluding any Annex A control.

Control titles below are the short names of the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls. The text of each control is not reproduced; see the standard.

## Justification codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rxx | Risk in the [risk register](6.1.2-risk-register.md) |
| EXT-xx, INT-xx | Issue in [4.1](../clause-04-context/4.1-internal-external-issues.md) |
| IP-xx | Interested party requirement in [4.2](../clause-04-context/4.2-interested-parties.md) |
| LEG | Legal requirement: Law No. 2019-014 on personal data protection, Law No. 2018-026 on cybersecurity and cybercrime |
| BP | Good practice, or needed to run the ISMS, with no specific risk |

## Implementation status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Implemented | In place and working; evidence available |
| Partial | Something exists but does not meet the planned control |
| Planned | Not in place; in the risk treatment plan |
