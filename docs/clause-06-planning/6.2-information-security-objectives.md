# 6.2 Information security objectives

Clause 6.2 asks for information security objectives that are consistent with the policy, measurable where possible, monitored, communicated and updated, and, for each, what will be done, with what resources, by whom, by when and how results are evaluated.

| ID | Objective | Measure and source | Target and date | Owner | What will be done | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OBJ-01 | Every staff account uses multi-factor authentication | Share of active Keycloak accounts with MFA set up, counted at the quarterly access review (POL-01 section 8) | 100% by 2027-01-31, then kept | Head of Infrastructure | Action A04 | R07 |
| OBJ-02 | Lagune Pay data can be restored within the recovery target | Quarterly restore test report (POL-06 section 4): time taken against the 4-hour target | One passing test every quarter from 2027-Q1 | Head of Infrastructure | Action A08 | R04 |
| OBJ-03 | Critical vulnerabilities in libraries used in production are fixed quickly | Dependency scan results in the pipeline: days from publication to fix | 100% fixed within 7 days, from 2027-04-30 | Head of Development | Action A10 | R09 |
| OBJ-04 | Failed control tests are dealt with | Evidence registers: days from a failed scheduled lab run to its entry with a cause | 100% within 5 working days, from 2026-11-01 | ISMS manager | Monitoring plan (9.1) | All tested controls |
| OBJ-05 | Staff recognise phishing | Attendance lists of awareness sessions; click rate of the test campaign | 2 sessions in 2027, attendance at least 90%; click rate lower in the second campaign than in the first | ISMS manager | Action A14 | R07 |
| OBJ-06 | The gaps found by IA-2026-01 are closed | Nonconformity log (10.2) | All 6 nonconformities closed, with effectiveness checked, by 2027-08-31 | ISMS manager | Corrective actions in the log | All |

## Monitoring

The ISMS manager reports progress on each objective in the monthly ISMS report (9.1) and at each management review. Objectives are reviewed every year at the management review.
