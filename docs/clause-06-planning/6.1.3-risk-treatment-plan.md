# 6.1.3 e) and f) Risk treatment plan

Clause 6.1.3 e) requires a risk treatment plan. Clause 6.1.3 f) requires the risk owners to approve the plan and to accept the residual risks. Clause 6.2 b) asks how objectives will be achieved: who, what resources, when; this plan answers that for the controls in the [Statement of Applicability](6.1.3-statement-of-applicability.md).

Every control marked Planned or Partial in the SoA is covered by at least one action below. Costs are estimates for the fictional company, in XOF, excluding staff time; effort is in person-days.

## Priorities

Deadlines follow the risk levels in the [risk register](6.1.2-risk-register.md): actions for the Critical risk (R01) are due by December 2026; actions for High risks by April 2027; the internal audit takes place in July 2027, before the stage 1 certification audit planned for September 2027.

## Follow-up

The ISMS manager updates the Status column every month (Not started, In progress, Done, Late) and reports late actions to the CEO. When an action is Done, the evidence goes in the `evidence/` folder and the SoA status of its controls changes to Implemented. The residual risk is then checked again in the risk register.
