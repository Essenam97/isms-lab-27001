# 6.1.2 Risk register

Method: [6.1.2-risk-assessment-method.md](6.1.2-risk-assessment-method.md). L = likelihood, I = impact, S = score. "Target" is the residual risk expected once the planned controls work; it is checked again after implementation.

| ID | Risk scenario | Assets | Source | Owner | Existing controls | L | I | S | Level | Option | Planned controls (Annex A) | Target L x I = S |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | An attacker phishes a customer's payroll administrator, logs in to Lagune Pay and changes employees' bank details; salaries are paid to the attacker. | Lagune Pay, payroll data | EXT-05, IP-01, IP-08 | Head of Development | Password login only | 4 | 5 | 20 | Critical | Modify | MFA for customer admin accounts (8.5); email and SMS alert to the employee on each bank detail change, change applied after 48 h (8.16); log of all bank detail changes (8.15) | 2 x 4 = 8 |
| R02 | A developer queries or edits the production database for debugging and leaks or corrupts customer data. | Production database | INT-02 | Head of Infrastructure | None; developers share one database account | 3 | 5 | 15 | High | Modify | Remove developer access to production (8.2, 8.3); separate test data with fake records (8.31, 8.33); break-glass access with approval and logging (5.18, 8.15) | 1 x 5 = 5 |
| R03 | A support agent views or exports data of customers whose tickets they do not handle. | Lagune Pay, payroll data | INT-03 | Head of Customer Support | Confidentiality clause in contracts | 3 | 4 | 12 | High | Modify | Support role limited to the customer of the open ticket (5.15, 8.3); masked bank and ID numbers in the support view (8.11); monthly review of access logs (8.15) | 2 x 3 = 6 |
| R04 | Data is lost (deletion, ransomware, provider failure) and the backup cannot be restored. | Production database, backups | INT-07 | Head of Infrastructure | Daily backups at the same provider, never restored | 3 | 5 | 15 | High | Modify | Copy of backups at a second provider (8.13); quarterly restore test with a written report (8.13, 5.30) | 1 x 5 = 5 |
| R05 | NimbusHost is unavailable for over 4 hours during the payroll window. | Production servers | EXT-07, IP-01 | Head of Infrastructure | Provider SLA of 99.9% | 2 | 4 | 8 | Medium | Modify | Documented recovery plan to a second provider with a tested recovery time (5.30); supplier monitoring (5.22) | 2 x 3 = 6 |
| R06 | Personal data is hosted outside Togo without a documented legal basis; the IPDCP opens an investigation. | Payroll data | EXT-01, EXT-02, IP-04 | CEO | None | 3 | 4 | 12 | High | Modify | Register of legal requirements (5.31); processing declaration to the IPDCP and transfer analysis (5.34); data protection clauses in the NimbusHost contract (5.20) | 1 x 4 = 4 |
| R07 | A Lagune Payroll employee is phished; the attacker takes over their account on internal tools. | Keycloak, Nextcloud, Gitea, email | EXT-05 | Head of Infrastructure | Passwords; no MFA | 4 | 4 | 16 | High | Modify | MFA for all staff through Keycloak (5.17, 8.5); phishing awareness sessions twice a year (6.3); alert on logins from new countries (8.16) | 2 x 4 = 8 |
| R08 | A laptop with source code or customer exports is lost or stolen. | Laptops, source code | INT-05 | Head of Infrastructure | Login password | 3 | 4 | 12 | High | Modify | Full-disk encryption (8.1, 8.24); ban on local customer exports (5.10); remote wipe (8.1) | 3 x 2 = 6 |
| R09 | Fast releases put a vulnerable change in production (for example SQL injection) that exposes customer data. | Lagune Pay, source code | INT-04 | Head of Development | Code review by one peer, not mandatory | 3 | 5 | 15 | High | Modify | Mandatory peer review in Gitea before merge (8.25, 8.28); automated security tests in the pipeline (8.29); dependency scanning (8.8) | 2 x 4 = 8 |
| R10 | A power cut or fibre outage stops the support team during the payroll window. | Office network, support team | EXT-06, EXT-08 | Head of Infrastructure | 4G backup router | 4 | 2 | 8 | Medium | Modify | UPS for network equipment (7.11); written remote-work fallback for support staff (5.29) | 2 x 2 = 4 |
| R11 | A personal phone used for email and MFA is lost or stolen. | Staff phones, email | INT-05 | Head of Infrastructure | Phone screen lock left to the user | 3 | 3 | 9 | Medium | Modify | Mobile device rules in the remote working and BYOD policy (6.7, 8.1); screen lock and remote sign-out required for email access | 2 x 2 = 4 |
| R12 | A departing employee keeps access to internal tools or Lagune Pay admin. | All internal tools | IP-05 | HR Manager | Manual offboarding email to IT | 3 | 4 | 12 | High | Modify | Offboarding checklist triggered by HR (6.5); accounts disabled centrally in Keycloak (5.18); quarterly access review (5.18) | 1 x 4 = 4 |
| R13 | Flooding damages equipment at the Lomé office. | Office equipment | EXT-08 | CEO | Equipment on the first floor | 2 | 2 | 4 | Low | Retain | None | 2 x 2 = 4 |
